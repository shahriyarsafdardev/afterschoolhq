About AfterSchool HQ.

With the mission of engaging students in activities that ignite their passions, AfterSchool HQ (AHQ) provides an online platform that connects students with programs, both virtual and in their community. For activity programs, AHQ offers management tools to create a free profile, promote activities, enroll students, communicate with parents, and more. And for parents, the database of programs provides a central location to discover, register, and even pay for activities for their children. By supporting both programs and parents, AfterSchool HQ works to cultivate exceptional learning opportunities for all students, everywhere.

Darye Henry

Darye is the co-founder and CEO of AfterSchool HQ. He is an Indianapolis native and has worked as an entrepreneur in the software field for over 20 years. Darye has worked with numerous startups as a CTO, helping non-technical founders build technology companies. He loves to create technology with a purpose and has a passion to impact the education space in ways that enable more students to have access to quality education. He has also served many years the non-profit world working with students looking to give back to his community.

San Pathak

San is the co-founder and COO of AfterSchool HQ, a startup focused on enabling students to find their vocation by transforming how parents find learning experiences for their children outside of the school day. A TechPoint Tech 25 honoree, San has a deep background in technology as well as business operations and specializes in driving discipline in order to unlock business potential and achieve visionary goals.
