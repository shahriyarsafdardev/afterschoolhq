What We Do For Schools & Providers

AfterSchool HQ aims to get more students in after-school activities through our online platform that allows parents to find, register, and pay for activities.

Showcase programs

Showcase all of your activities on a customizable profile page. If parents see something their student would like, they can register while it’s still top of mind.

Collect registrations

Rather than sending home paperwork that might get left in a student’s backpack, keep registration simple. After parents register their student(s) once, their information will be saved so that they can register for the next program with just a couple of clicks!

Process online payment

No more lost cash or checks! Offer parents the ability to pay online with either credit card or ACH and customize payment options, such as subscriptions or payment plans.
