About AfterSchool HQ Sponsorships

Help make afterschool activities possible for the students in your area. The sponsorship you provide will enable a school to begin using the AfterSchool HQ platform to manage, promote, and track their afterschool activities. In return, your company’s brand will appear on all registration forms of your sponsored school.

AfterSchool HQ sponsorships are annual plans, but you are under no obligation to continue sponsoring after the first year. Sponsorships come in two tiers and are based on the number of students in a school. To find out more information on pricing and how you can get involved, fill out the form below!
