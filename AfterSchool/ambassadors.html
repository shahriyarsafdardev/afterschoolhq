We’re enabling students to find their passion and calling by transforming how parents find learning experiences for their children outside of the school day.

Help Us Transform Education

Because of the opportunities afforded to us through activity programs, we have ambitious goals for AfterSchool HQ. We aim to give students the same range of opportunity so they can explore their skills and interests. When students are given options, they have the chance to find their passions and dream big.

AfterSchool HQ Ambassador Program

AfterSchool AQ Ambassadors are advocates for after-school activities, summer camps, and weekend programs. As an Ambassador, you will engage with schools and providers in your network, leading them to set up profiles on AfterSchool HQ.

How It Works 

1. You refer schools or service providers via your custom URL
2. Schools or providers sign up, create activities on the platform, and begin enrollment
3. You receive a percentage of the transaction fees that are collected 

It’s that easy! And it really does make a difference. The sooner a child finds their calling, the sooner they can start walking in their purpose. We’d like your help to make this possible.
