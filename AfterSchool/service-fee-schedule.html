AfterSchool HQ Service Fee Schedule

When you enroll in or register for an activity with a provider, you agree to be bound by and to pay for that transaction. All activities listed, paid, and processed through AfterSchool HQ are subject to processing fees. AfterSchool HQ charges an after-school program support fee on top for each transaction, along with a transaction fee if the customer pays with a credit card. Therefore, the list price to customers will be greater than the price entered by the activity provider.
